- Single-page portfolio with in-page navigation across the home, about, education, experience, projects, skills, and contact sections.

- Preserve the supplied biography, education and experience details, project descriptions, technology lists, project links, and contact destinations. Do not invent additional claims or proof.
- The portfolio is Indonesian-first while retaining supplied English technology and role terminology.

## Brand Commitments

- The supplied profile photograph is the personal portrait asset.
